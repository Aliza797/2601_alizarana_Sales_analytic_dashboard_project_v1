# Business Insights

## Revenue & Profitability
- Total revenue exceeded $2.29M.
- Around 18% of transactions were loss-making.

---

## Discount Analysis
- Higher discounts negatively impacted profitability.
- Discounts above 20% showed significantly lower average profit.

---

## Category Performance
- Technology category generated the highest overall profit.
- Furniture category showed relatively weaker profitability.

---

## Regional Analysis
- West region generated the highest sales revenue.
- Central region showed comparatively lower profitability.

---

## Product-Level Insights
- Certain products consistently generated large losses.
- Loss-making products may require pricing or discount strategy review.

---

## Operational Insights
- Average shipping time was approximately 4 days.
- Shipping performance appeared relatively consistent across orders.

---

## Recommendations
- Reduce excessive discounting on low-margin products.
- Review pricing strategy for loss-making products.
- Focus on high-performing regions and profitable categories.
